    How it works

    1. Data. 1000×1000 annotated patches of Paris maps (1760–1994), split by source map so no map appears in both training and test.
    2. Model. A 3-level U-Net with skip connections, BatchNorm and bilinear upsampling, trained with AdamW on Apple Silicon (MPS).
    3. Deploy. Exported to ONNX and checked against PyTorch (identical predictions), then run here with ONNX Runtime Web (WebAssembly).

    Data: Historical City Maps Semantic Segmentation Dataset (Petitpierre, 2021), CC BY-NC 4.0. Sample tiles and model weights are for non-commercial use. Code: MIT.
